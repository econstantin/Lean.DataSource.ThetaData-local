# Audit of the ThetaData connector

Baseline: `master` at `d9e4e04` ("Run CI on self-hosted runners (#19)"), the
same commit as `QuantConnect/Lean.DataSource.ThetaData` master on 2026-10-28.
The changes are on the `patched` branch. The audit covers every
source file, the tests, the CI file, the README, and `thetadata.json`.

"Free plan check" means a request to a local Theta Terminal v3 on a Free
account on 2026-10-28. "Offline test" means a test in
`QuantConnect.ThetaData.Tests` that runs on recorded responses, with no
terminal.

## Performance

- 1 day of a whole chain is 1 request. For SPY on 2026-10-20 it was 5 MB in
  about 20 s, and 12,726 contracts. The quote and trade requests of the same
  day share it.
- A history request is split by the v3 limits only: 1 day for an interval under
  1 minute, 28 days for 1 minute or more, and 365 days for end-of-day data
  (F33).
- The plan's concurrency limit applies to the whole client. The canonical
  intraday download runs at most `thetadata-max-concurrent-requests` contracts
  at once, through a queue of at most 100,000 items.
- Each response is read into memory before parsing. A whole SPXW day is 8 MB of
  JSON. That fits, but a streaming parser would use less memory.

## Not covered

- A paid plan: intraday, tick, open interest, the contract list, and streaming.
- Live trading and `lean live deploy`.
- Load tests of the concurrency limit against a real terminal.
